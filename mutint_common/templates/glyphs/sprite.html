{% comment %} The glyph sprite: one per name in mutint_common.glyphs.GLYPHS, each in a 16x16 box, drawn once per page and referenced by from wherever a mutation is drawn as a shape -- mutint-recurrent's cells, legend and gene plots, mutint-circos's rings. The paths carry no fill or stroke of their own: the referencing element decides. The tombstone is a half-circle on a rectangle, the bowtie two triangles meeting at a point, the barbell two triangles pointing outward from a bar. `data-glyph-sprite` is how a script finds the symbols to copy into a downloaded file. {% endcomment %}
