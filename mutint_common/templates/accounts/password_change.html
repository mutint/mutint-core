{% extends 'base.html' %} {% block title %}Change password{% endblock %} {% block header %}Change password{% endblock %} {% comment %} Django's own PasswordChangeView and PasswordChangeForm, rendered in this product's shell rather than in Django admin's. The sidebar used to link /admin/password_change/, which is wrapped in `AdminSite.admin_view` -- so an ordinary user was bounced to the admin login and told to "enter the correct username and password for a staff account". The link was broken for exactly the people most likely to click it, and worked for staff only by leaving the product entirely. The fields are written out by hand, as every form in this repo is, rather than through `{{ form }}` or a form-rendering tag library. django-bootstrap3 and django-bootstrap4 were both installed once and no template ever loaded either, so they are gone; this page is not the place to reintroduce one. What comes off the bound form is the errors and the help text; `name=` is the contract with PasswordChangeForm and is not ours to rename. There is deliberately no `value=` on any of them. Nothing here validates a password. `PasswordChangeForm.clean_new_password2` calls `validate_password()`, which is what applies AUTH_PASSWORD_VALIDATORS, and `PasswordChangeView.form_valid` calls `update_session_auth_hash`, which is what stops a person logging themselves out by changing their own password. {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
