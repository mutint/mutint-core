An experiment whose ancestor was never sequenced still has one. The mutations present in every population and every sample are its starting line rather than evolution. This page finds them, lets you choose which to keep, and makes a sample carrying them that becomes the experiment's ancestor: excluded from every view and analysis, its mutations subtracted from every other sample.

The sliders relax the rule. A mutation is shared when samples from at least the chosen number of populations carry it and at least the chosen fraction of all samples do. Each copied mutation is recorded as fixed in the new sample, and the copy appears in the Curate history, where it can be undone.
