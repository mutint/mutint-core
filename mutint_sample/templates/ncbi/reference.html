The type controls how the sequence is used by plugins that call mutations:

  • Reference – Mutations are called in this completely assembled chromosome or plasmid.
  • Contig – Mutations are called in contigs from an incomplete assembly. Contigs are interpreted as pieces of the same chromosome or plasmid. Mutations near contig ends may be censored by mutation callers to avoid artifacts, and mutation callers may assume that all contigs in a set have the same read-depth coverage.
  • Junction-only – An additional engineered sequence, like an integration plasmid or a transposon. Mutations are not called in this sequence, but insertions of pieces of this sequence into other references may be reported.
